AI-Powered HR & Workforce Management Platform

Designed a scalable HR SaaS platform designed to simplify workforce management through a clean, data-driven user experience.

From employee management and attendance tracking to recruitment, performance analytics, payroll, and leave management, every interface was designed to reduce complexity while improving productivity.

The project includes responsive web, tablet, and mobile experiences supported by a scalable design system, reusable components, and a structured information architecture for enterprise-ready product growth.

HR Communication Hub

Designed a centralized inbox that streamlines internal HR communication, employee requests, and administrative workflows within a single workspace.

The interface enables HR teams to review leave requests, employee updates, interview coordination, and system notifications without switching between multiple tools.

A structured three-panel layout, clear visual hierarchy, smart labels, and distraction-free reading experience help users process conversations faster while maintaining focus on critical HR operations.

The design improves communication efficiency while supporting scalable enterprise workforce management.

HR Planning & Scheduling Calendar

Designed a centralized scheduling experience that helps HR teams manage recruitment, onboarding, employee development, performance reviews, training sessions, and company events from a single calendar. The interface combines flexible filtering, categorized agendas, and detailed event panels to improve planning, reduce scheduling conflicts, and keep teams aligned across departments.

The clean calendar layout, structured information hierarchy, and enterprise-focused workflow enable HR managers to organize high-volume activities efficiently while maintaining complete visibility into workforce operations.

Employee Management &
Workforce Directory

Designed a centralized employee management experience that gives HR teams complete visibility into workforce data through a clean, structured, and highly scannable interface. The dashboard combines employee records, department distribution, hiring trends, turnover insights, employment status, and advanced filtering in a single workspace, allowing teams to manage growing organizations more efficiently.

The design emphasizes clear data hierarchy, fast search, reusable table patterns, and enterprise-scale usability, making day-to-day workforce administration faster, more organized, and easier to scale.

Employee Directory & Workforce Profiles

Designed a visual employee directory that provides HR teams with a faster and more intuitive way to browse, search, and manage workforce information.

Instead of relying solely on traditional data tables, this card-based layout presents employee profiles, departments, employment types, work models, and availability in a highly scannable format.

Advanced search, filtering, and status indicators help managers quickly identify the right people while improving navigation across large organizations.

The interface balances visual clarity with enterprise-scale usability, creating a more efficient experience for everyday HR operations.
