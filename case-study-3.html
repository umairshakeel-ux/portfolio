AI-Powered Logistics & Fleet Management Platform

Navix AI is an AI-powered Logistics SaaS and Fleet Management Platform designed in Figma for complex transportation operations. I designed the UI/UX across web and mobile, covering logistics dashboards, shipment management, real-time tracking, fleet management, warehouse operations, route optimization, analytics, and AI automation. The product focuses on simplifying data-heavy workflows, improving operational visibility, and helping logistics teams make faster, informed decisions.

Shipment Management Dashboard

Designed a streamlined shipment management dashboard for a logistics SaaS platform, giving operations teams a clear view of active shipments, destinations, delivery status, assigned drivers, ETA, and shipment value.

The interface uses structured data tables, status indicators, search, and quick actions to help teams monitor deliveries, identify delays, and manage day-to-day logistics operations efficiently.

UX Focus: Shipment Management • Logistics Dashboard • SaaS UI/UX • Data Table Design • Fleet Operations • Figma

Fleet Management & AI Vehicle Intelligence

Designed a fleet management dashboard for an AI-powered logistics SaaS platform, giving operations teams real-time visibility into vehicles, drivers, routes, load capacity, fuel usage, maintenance, and fleet health. The experience combines live vehicle tracking, predictive maintenance alerts, AI-powered fleet insights, and operational data to help logistics teams identify critical issues, reduce downtime, optimize fleet utilization, and make faster decisions from one centralized dashboard.

UX Focus: Fleet Management • Logistics SaaS • AI Dashboard • Vehicle Tracking • Data Visualization • Predictive Maintenance • Figma
