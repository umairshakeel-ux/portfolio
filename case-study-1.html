Virtual Office & Meeting Management SaaS | Product Design & UI/UX

A modern B2B SaaS workspace designed to help distributed teams schedule meetings, manage virtual rooms, track activity, monitor workspace usage, and collaborate from one centralized product experience.

Overview

Remote and hybrid teams often rely on multiple tools to manage meetings, calendars, team availability, virtual rooms, workspace activity, and account information.

This product brings those workflows together into a single virtual office and meeting management SaaS platform.

I designed the product experience around two priorities:

  • Make meeting activity immediately understandable
  • Give users one place to manage daily collaboration without jumping between disconnected tools

The result is a scalable SaaS interface combining meeting scheduling, live sessions, upcoming meetings, virtual rooms, team presence, calendar management, workspace analytics, and account activity.

Meeting Management Dashboard
Scheduling & Virtual Collaboration

Problem:

Users needed a faster way to understand what was happening across their workspace without opening individual meetings or switching between multiple calendar and communication tools.

Live sessions, future meetings, and completed activity needed to remain visually distinct while still feeling part of one connected workflow.

Solution:

I designed a centralized meeting management interface that separates activity into:

  • Live Meetings
  • Upcoming Meetings
  • Past Meetings

Each meeting card surfaces the information users need before joining:

  • Meeting title
  • Date and time
  • Organization or workspace
  • Participant avatars
  • Meeting capacity
  • Status
  • Confirmation state

High-priority actions such as Schedule Meeting remain immediately accessible, while a persistent member panel provides visibility into team presence.

The card-based system also creates a flexible foundation for future meeting filters, search, recurring sessions, invitations, and team management features.

UX Outcome

The screen reduces the amount of navigation required to understand meeting activity and creates a clear meeting lifecycle:

Schedule → Confirm → Join → Complete → Review

B2B SaaS Dashboard | Virtual Office &
Workspace Management

Problem:

Users needed a single overview of their daily workspace activity, but displaying meetings, account status, revenue, storage, rooms, members, and calendar information together could easily create an overloaded dashboard.

The challenge was deciding what deserves immediate attention and what should remain secondary.

Solution:

I designed a modular SaaS dashboard that combines operational information with high-frequency actions.

The dashboard includes:

  • Profile completion status
  • Daily meeting activity
  • Booked meetings
  • Monthly income
  • Workspace storage usage
  • Upcoming meetings
  • Calendar and schedule
  • Virtual rooms
  • Join Room action
  • Create New Room action
  • Team member presence

The layout uses clear hierarchy and modular dashboard widgets so users can scan key information quickly without losing access to detailed workflows.

The calendar remains visible alongside meeting activity, helping users connect upcoming events with their daily schedule instead of treating them as separate systems.

UX Outcome

The dashboard creates a clear operational command center for the virtual workspace:

Monitor → Schedule → Join → Collaborate → Manage

Expert Profile & Meeting Booking
SaaS Scheduling UX

Problem:

Users needed a simple way to discover people inside the workspace, understand their background, and book time with them without leaving the platform or moving between a profile page and a separate scheduling tool.

The challenge was combining personal profile content, availability, meeting duration, pricing, time zones, and calendar scheduling in one experience without making the screen feel overloaded.

Solution:

I designed a unified People Profile and Meeting Booking experience that connects professional discovery with scheduling.

The screen includes:

  • Expert / team member profile
  • Bio and background information
  • Meeting duration and pricing
  • Calendar availability
  • Available time slots
  • Time zone selection
  • Personal schedule visibility
  • Blocked and focus-time states
  • Direct booking workflow

The layout keeps profile context on the left while the booking interaction stays immediately accessible on the right, helping users move naturally from:

Discover → Evaluate → Check Availability → Select Time → Book

UX Outcome

The experience reduces friction between discovering the right person and scheduling a meeting with them. Instead of treating profiles and calendars as separate workflows, both are connected inside one focused SaaS interaction.

This also expands the platform beyond meeting management into a broader professional networking, expert booking, team collaboration, and virtual workspace experience.
